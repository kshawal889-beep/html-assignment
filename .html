medicine

Medicine is the science and practice of diagnosing, treating, and preventing disease, alongside the substances (drugs, pills) used to cure or ease symptoms. It encompasses both the study of health and the administration of treatment to restore well-being. Common usage includes taking, prescribing, or swallowing medicine.

surgery

Surgery is a branch of medicine that treats diseases, injuries, or deformities through manual and instrumental methods, typically involving cutting, removing, or altering tissues, organs, or bones. Performed by trained surgeons, these operations can range from minor procedures to complex interventions designed to diagnose, repair, or improve a patient's physical health.

DNA

DNA, or deoxyribonucleic acid, is the hereditary material in humans and almost all living organisms that contains the genetic instructions for development, functioning, growth, and reproduction. It is a double-stranded molecule structured as a double helix (twisted ladder) located in the cell nucleus and mitochondria.
