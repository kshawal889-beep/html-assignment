ingredients

Noodles: 450g fresh or dried noodles (Shanghai, chow mein, or ramen). Sauce: 3 tbsp light soy sauce, 2.5 tbsp dark soy sauce, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tbsp sesame oil, 4 tbsp water/stock. Aromatics: 6 cloves garlic (sliced), 3 green onions. Vegetables/Protein: Bean sprouts, choy sum, or sliced onions.

instruction

Prepare Noodles: Boil noodles until just cooked, then rinse immediately with cold water to remove starch and prevent sticking. Make Sauce: Combine soy sauces, sugar, sesame oil, and stock in a bowl. Stir-fry: Heat oil in a wok over high heat. Sauté aromatics (garlic, onion) for 1 minute. Combine: Add noodles and vegetables, tossing for 2-3 minutes. Pour in sauce and toss until noodles are evenly coated and charred, about 3-4 minutes.
